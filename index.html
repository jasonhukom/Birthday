Leave, Stay Forever

Dataset 01

Whatever transpires When it rains, and thunders I will always stand still with you Remaining sheltered under my broken roof With you I am sheltered With you I am calm I do not want to leave I am wet, and cold in the rain I will let you go Don't think I'll leave Because in the end Everything will be worth it After the rain stops After the thunder stops The sky will be blue And there will be sunshine and rainbow

Too Kind

Dataset 02

Generosity is nothing without a price Even warmth demands a sacrifice Losing your whole world entirely for them Despite the fact that they don't care Searching for someone kind Is like searching for a feeling that doesn't exist People hurt each other, no one is civil Kind-hearted individuals are hard to find With all the hate and the cruelty of this world We should all encourage ourselves to be better To be kind to wicked souls Cruelty paid with spite simply fractures the world further You should be kind Be the kindest person in the room For every harsh thing they did, Respond to them with kindness beyond their thoughts When they're harsh Give them warmth Sometimes a good deed doesn't need to be paid back Being kind to them unconditionally, without receiving anything back

My Princess

Dataset 03

Every morning and every night. Somewhere between the passing hours, I find my thoughts wandering back to you Since the day you entered my life time feels different. Seconds become minutes, Minutes become hours, Yet every conversation with you still feels far too short Across the land and the sea, Across every mile between us, You've become a part of my heart, A part where that distance cannot reach, A part forever attached You bring me a kind of comfort That is difficult to put into words The sort that turns ordinary days Into something worth looking forward to I never expected we'd grow this close, but I still remember the day we met. It's crystal clear, if I have 9 lives, I think I'd choose to meet you all over again. You are a diamond to my wooden pickaxe, Someone I want but can't have. If I don't have you with me. I am just a lonely boy all over again I'm the Red King to my Princess. You are the Mist in the Winter. The level that's wise and kind. You'll always be my favorite note. And though words often fall short, there is one thing I know for certain talking to you every day has become one of my favorite parts of my day

Breaking Itself Apart

Dataset 04

Do you know what a lit candle feels? A lit candle seems to brighten your darkness The flames that accompany us But the liquid amber of one candle suffers while it lights up Just for you, the candle melts It melts to light up your darkness Piece by piece Until there's nothing left Once a beacon of hope, Now dissolved into useless substance Mankind used Mankind threw Everything is only useful until they part It breaks them apart Like a candle Melting itself

Happiness

Dataset 05

Happiness isn't a big thing It's a text back at 4am It's a song stuck in your head That you don't even mind It's remembering an inside joke out of nowhere, for no reason Laughing alone at your phone Like it's nobody's business Sometimes it's just a normal day Nothing special happening at all But somehow it still feels good Because someone makes it feel that way I used to think happiness was big moments Trophies, achievements, perfect days Now I think it's smaller than that It's just having someone who stays

June 2026

Dataset 06

June felt like something I didn't want to end I kept thinking about it even after it was over Wishing I could rewind it back Like pressing replay on a good song Then July came around And I didn't expect much from it But somehow it turned worst from usually is Less talks, calls, game I don't think July can ever replace June It's not supposed to It's not going to It's different Some months just stick with you Not because everything was perfect But because of the little conversations That made the days feel lighter

Her Curiosity

entry 08

You always have another question Before I even finish answering the first one About languages, about worlds, about things Most people stop wondering about You ask why, and then why again Not to annoy anyone Just because you actually want to know Because your brain doesn't like leaving things unfinished I think that's just who you are Someone who wants to understand everything Even the small stuff Even the things that don't matter to anyone else There's always another question with you Another thing to learn Another place you want to imagine And honestly, I think that's my favorite part of you
